How to use a focus block

Keep the tool simple so it helps rather than distracts.

1. Pick one task

Choose a specific task from your Study Planner.

2. Focus until the timer ends

Avoid switching to unrelated tasks during the session.

3. Review and rest

Use the break, then decide whether another session is useful.
